Myungli Diary: English review sheet. Thank you for reviewing. Myungli Diary is a diary app that reads each day through saju (the Korean Four Pillars of Destiny) and asks one question to start your diary. Every line in this sheet is shown to users in the app, on the store page, or on the website. Please read each line as a native speaker and tell us where it sounds wrong, unnatural, or unclear.

Each tab is one group of lines. You only edit three columns: Suggestion, Category, and Note. All grey columns are for reference. Please do not add, delete, or reorder rows. Sorting and filtering are fine.

Columns. Key: the internal name of the line. Bundle: the group it belongs to. Where it appears: the screen or place (written in Korean for the writer), then the length limit for English. Korean original: the source sentence. Current translation: the English line as it is now, including placeholders. Example: only for lines with placeholders or term marks. It shows the line with sample values filled in (a date, a time, an event title), so you can read it as users will. It is empty when the line has nothing to fill. Suggestion: your corrected line. Category: why you changed it. Note: anything you want to tell us. Writer decision: leave this empty.

Voice and tone. The app speaks like a calm diary that looks at your day with you. Plain statements. Imperatives only when giving a piece of advice. Specific rather than abstract: say what, when, and how. Warm where the line is meant to comfort, but never dramatic. No exclamation marks, no emoji, no ellipses. The user is addressed by first name only, at most twice a week, never as "dear" or with a title.

Please keep placeholders exactly as they are. Anything in curly braces, such as {name}, {date} or {count}, is replaced by the app. You may move a placeholder within the sentence, but do not rename, translate, add, or remove it. Lines with {count, plural, one{...} other{...}} have one form for 1 and one for other numbers. Keep that structure and edit only the words inside each form.

Terms. Saju terms are written in romanized Korean (for example Bigyeop, Siksang), because the app explains them one level down. Earthly branches use zodiac animals (Tiger, Rabbit). Please do not replace a romanized term with an English translation. If a term itself reads wrong, choose Terminology and explain in Note. The terms tab in a later round covers the names themselves. In Current translation a term is marked like [[Bigyeop|ten_god_group.bigyeop]]. In your suggestion you can write just Bigyeop; we put the mark back. Keep the term spelled the same so we can find it.

Length. If "Where it appears" shows a limit (for example Max 40 characters), your suggestion must fit within it, counting the filled-in example. We cannot apply a suggestion that is longer. If you cannot fit a good line within the limit, write your best line anyway and say so in Note.

Category values. Mistranslation: the meaning differs from the Korean original. Unnatural: the meaning is right, but a native speaker would not say it this way. Grammar: grammar, spelling, or punctuation. Terminology: the same thing is called by different names, or a term is wrong.

Characters to avoid: the em dash, the en dash, and the middle dot. Use commas, "and", or "to" instead.

Example. Current translation: "Your efforts are likely to bear fruit today." Suggestion: "Your effort is likely to pay off today." Category: Unnatural. Note: "Bear fruit" sounds a little formal for an everyday app.
