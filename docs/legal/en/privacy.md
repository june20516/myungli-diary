# Privacy Policy

Effective date: (to be set on confirmation)

{{business.name}} (the "Company") processes and protects the personal information of people who use the Myungli Diary app and its services (the "Service") in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act (「개인정보 보호법」) and other applicable laws. This policy explains what personal information the Company processes and why, how long it is kept, and what rights users have.

## 1. Purposes of Processing Personal Information

The Company processes personal information only for the purposes below. If a purpose changes, the Company will give advance notice as required by law and obtain consent where necessary.

1. **Sign-up and account management:** confirming the intent to sign up, identifying the user, keeping the user signed in, linking multiple sign-in methods and merging accounts, and processing account deletion
2. **Providing the Service:** calculating the user's saju (Four Pillars) and the fortune of each day, saving diary entries and schedules, providing today's question and sentences, and sending morning notifications
3. **Providing paid services:** verifying ad-free pass purchases, managing the period of use, and handling purchase restoration and refunds
4. **Advertising:** showing ads to free users, and unlocking the basis view in return for watching a rewarded ad
5. **Responding to inquiries:** reviewing inquiries, replying, and keeping records of handling
6. **Service stability and abuse prevention:** analyzing the causes of crashes, managing request limits, preventing repeated use of the first reading and fraudulent receipt of rewards, and responding to security incidents

## 2. Personal Information Processed

### Information the user enters

| Category | Items |
| --- | --- |
| Saju information (required) | Date of birth, solar or lunar calendar and whether it is a leap month, time of birth (left blank if unknown), gender, time zone of birth |
| Saju information (optional) | City of birth (only if entered) |

From the sign-in method the user chooses among Kakao, LINE, Google, and Apple, the Company receives that method's user identifier. The Company does not receive the user's name, email address, phone number, or profile photo. When the user signs in with Apple, the Company also keeps the refresh token issued by Apple.

### Information generated automatically while using the Service

| Item | Used for |
| --- | --- |

| Device information (OS, model), app version | Responding to inquiries, crash analysis |

## 3. Retention and Use Period

The Company destroys personal information as soon as its purpose has been achieved. However, the information below is kept for the period stated.

| Information | Period |
| --- | --- |
| Saju information, profile, settings, sign-in method links | Until account deletion (destroyed immediately upon deletion) |
| Diary entries and schedules | Content is destroyed 30 days after the user deletes it, or immediately upon account deletion |
| Records of the day's question, tags, and the day's character | Until account deletion |
| Sentence reactions | Until account deletion (after deletion, only per-sentence totals that cannot be traced to anyone remain) |
| Terms agreement records | Until account deletion |
| Saju calculation results (server cache) | 30 days after creation |
| Device token for notifications | Until account deletion. For devices no longer in use, such as after signing out, destroyed after 30 days |
| Morning notification sending records | 30 days |
| Sign-in token | Until it expires (some for 1 day after expiry) |
| IP address of requests sent without signing in (transformed value) | Up to 1 day |
| First reading installation id (transformed value) | 1 year |
| Rewarded ad viewing records | 1 year (regardless of account deletion, to prevent fraudulent receipt) |
| Inquiry emails | 3 years after handling is complete |
| Server backups | 7 days (destroyed information also remains in backup copies for this period before being erased) |

**Information kept as required by law**

| Information | Period | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |

## 4. Procedure and Method of Destroying Personal Information

Personal information whose retention period has ended or whose purpose has been achieved is destroyed without delay. Electronic files are erased by a method that makes recovery impossible, and no paper documents are created. Information that must be kept by law is stored separately from other information and destroyed when that period ends.

## 5. Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties

The Company does not provide users' personal information to third parties. The exceptions are cases with a basis in law, or where an investigative agency requests it following the procedure set by law.

For ads, Google AdMob receives and processes the advertising identifier and other information directly within the app. Google processes this information under its own responsibility, and details are in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). How to refuse the advertising identifier is described in Section 8.

## 8. Automatic Collection Tools and How to Refuse Them

The app does not use cookies. It uses the device's advertising identifier for ads. You can turn off or reset the advertising identifier as follows.

* **iPhone:** When the app asks for permission to track, choose "Ask App Not to Track", or turn it off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
* **Android:** Delete the advertising ID in Settings > Security & privacy (the name differs by device) > Ads.

If you refuse the advertising identifier, ads will still appear, but they will not be personalized.

## 9. Users' Rights and How to Exercise Them

Users may at any time request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information, or request that its processing be suspended.

* **Directly in the app:** You can change your saju information and name in Edit details on the Me tab, turn off notifications in Settings, and delete your account in Settings > Delete account. When you delete your account, your information is destroyed immediately, except the information kept by law as described in Section 3.
* **By inquiry:** If you make a request through Contact us in the app or by emailing the privacy officer below, it will be handled without delay.
* **Link records of sign-in methods:** For Kakao and Apple, when you delete your account or unlink a method, the Company also revokes its authorization for that service. For LINE and Google, a record may remain in the list of connected apps in each account's settings, and you can remove it yourself on that screen.

A legal representative or a person you have authorized may also exercise these rights. In that case, please also send materials that confirm the authorization.

## 10. Personal Information of Children Under 14

The Service is available only to people aged 14 or older. The Company does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14, and if it learns that it has collected such information, it destroys it immediately.

## 11. Measures to Ensure the Security of Personal Information

The Company takes the following measures to keep personal information secure.

* All communication between the app and the servers is encrypted (HTTPS), and databases and backups are stored in encrypted form.
* The service program has only permission to read and write data; changes to the database structure and operational settings can be made only with separately held permissions. Changes to operational settings are logged.
* Identifiers that do not need to be kept for long, such as IP addresses and installation ids, are stored as values that cannot be restored.
* Server request records keep only the path format of each request, not its content, and crash reports do not include diary text, saju information, or name.
